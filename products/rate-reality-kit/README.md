# The Rate Reality Kit

**$19 · Tier 1 of the RateTruth offer stack**

You just ran the calculator and saw the gap between what you bill and what you keep. This kit is the fix. Four documents, no fluff, all usable the same day you buy:

1. [`01-rate-setting-guide.md`](01-rate-setting-guide.md) — how to set a rate from your real numbers instead of guessing
2. [`02-proposal-template.md`](02-proposal-template.md) — a fill-in-the-blank proposal that sells on value, not hours
3. [`03-client-screening-checklist.md`](03-client-screening-checklist.md) — spot a bad client before you sign, not after
4. [`04-scope-creep-scripts.md`](04-scope-creep-scripts.md) — exact wording to push back when "one small thing" turns into five

## Who this is for

Freelancers who already have some client flow but are bleeding margin to underpricing, scope creep, or clients who were never going to pay well in the first place. Raising your rate is the single challenge freelancers report most often — this kit attacks that from four angles at once instead of one generic "charge more" pep talk.
